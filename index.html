HOW TO PLAY

Welcome to Campus Chaos! You will guide an engineering student through 4 pivotal years of college life.

📚
Academics: Attendance, internal exams, lab exams, and GPA. Keep it above 75% to avoid detention!
💻
Coding Skills: LeetCode, full-stack projects, and system design. High skills unlock top-tier tech roles.
⚡
Energy: Health, sleep, and sanity. If energy hits rock bottom, you risk burnout and penalty choices!
🤝
Relationships: Friends, club seniors, teamwork, and networking. Unlocks referrals and mental resilience.
💼
Career Readiness: Resumes, mock interviews, cold emails, and internship experience.

PRO-TIPS:

  • Every decision is a trade-off. You can't max out everything at once!
  • There are 5 distinct endings based on your stat distribution and milestones.
  • The game auto-saves after every choice in your browser's LocalStorage.
  • Press M anytime to toggle retro sound effects.
